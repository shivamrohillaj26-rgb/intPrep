2. What the * desugars to

<li *appFor="let city of cities(); let i = index">…</li>

↓ becomes

<ng-template
  [appForOf]="cities()"
  let-city
  let-i="index"
>
  <li>…</li>
</ng-template>

Input name must be appForOf when selector is [appFor] — the of in microsyntax maps to Of on the input.

Say in interview

  • Structural directives reshape the DOM: they get a TemplateRef (blueprint) and a ViewContainerRef (where to stamp views).
  • * is sugar over <ng-template>. *appFor="let x of list" → [appForOf]="list" + let-x.
  • On change I clear() and createEmbeddedView(tpl, { $implicit, index }). Real *ngFor / @for also diff + trackBy — this clone is the interview-simple version.
  • Attribute directive (Q10) styles/behavior on a host; structural directive adds/removes embedded views.
